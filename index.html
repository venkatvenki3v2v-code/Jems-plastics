Automated Production

Our 4-Step Blow Molding Process

Every PET bottle manufactured at JEMS PLASTICS undergoes automated stretch blow molding and strict quality checks.

01

Virgin Preforms

High-density 100% virgin PET resin preforms chosen for high tensile strength and food grade safety.

02

Infra-Red Heating

Controlled IR oven heating ensures uniform wall thickness distribution across all bottle neck sizes.

03

Stretch Molding

High-pressure pneumatic blow molds shape bottles with reinforced structural ribs for drop resistance.

04

Cap Testing & Dispatch

Leak testing with tamper-evident caps followed by hygienic bundling and regional freight dispatch.
